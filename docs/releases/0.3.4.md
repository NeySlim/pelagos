# Pelagos 0.3.4

Fixes for the security audit on the RPZ zones Pelagos generates, for configuration changes rolled back while BIND loads a large zone on a slow node, and for screens left on "Network error" until a reload. First release built, tested and drafted by the new release workflow; its container images also carry the fixes of 0.3.3. Nothing changes in the data of a cluster: an upgrade from 0.3.3 is a package update.

## Fixes
- **Security audit on the RPZ zones of the lists**: with an RPZ list or category enabled, **Installation > Security** reported every zone Pelagos generates for it (one per list, plus `pelagos-allow.rpz.invalid`) with a "transfer without read key" warning and a "static zone" note, and proposed `allow-transfer { key ddi-read; none; };` and an `update-policy`. Both proposals were harmful: the first reopens transfers of large policy zones, the second makes named write a journal next to files Pelagos rewrites. The audit, the import analysis and the adoption now skip the zones the cluster's RPZ settings generate. An RPZ zone of your own, declared in your files, is still audited.
- **Configuration change rolled back while BIND loads a large zone**: after `rndc reconfig`, named answers no control command until it has loaded the zones the change declares. On a slow node, enabling again an RPZ category whose list files were still on disk (18 MB, about 20 s to load) made the check after the change fail with "rndc status timed out", and the change was rolled back. The check now waits while named is busy, for a time that grows with the size of the zone files Pelagos manages (3 s per MB above the usual 15 s, at most 10 minutes). A refused connection, a refused key or an error still fails at once, and a reconfiguration that named never applied (its "last configured" time unchanged) is still rolled back.
- **"Network error" until a reload**: a read that failed on a short network cut (Wi-Fi, the web service restarting during an upgrade) left the screen on "Network error" until the page was reloaded. Reads (GET) are now retried after 0.5, 1 and 2 s on a network error or a 502, 503 or 504, then in the background (5 s to 60 s) and right away when the tab gets the focus back, the browser goes online or the live connection comes back; the error notice has a **Retry** button. Changes (POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE) are never retried by themselves. A screen whose code could not be downloaded (for instance after an upgrade replaced it) waits for the node to answer and reloads, instead of "Unexpected Application Error".

## Release process
- One `release` workflow, started by the version tag: full CI, debs (amd64 and arm64) and rpms (el9, el10) built and tested from the tag, blocking checks, a draft GitHub release holding the tested files (`release.json`, `NOTES.md`, `SHA256SUMS`), and the container images pushed to ghcr.io. The release is published by hand after review.
- `scripts/deploy-prod.sh --tag vX.Y.Z` takes the files of the GitHub release (draft included) and checks them against `SHA256SUMS` and `release.json` before upgrading.
- `docs/dev/release.md` describes the process.

## Tested
- **Upgrade 0.3.3 to 0.3.4 with `scripts/deploy-prod.sh`** on an HA pair reproducing a production cluster: Debian 13, BIND 9.20, Kea migrated from 2.6.3 to ISC Kea 3.2.1, HA hot-standby over TLS, nodes naming their interface differently, the RPZ security category enabled with its real lists (HaGeZi threat intelligence medium, 18 MB, about 322,000 names, plus scams and fakes), a DHCP client renewing every 3 s and a probe asking every second during the whole upgrade. Both nodes upgraded in 103 s, the pair answered 113 of 114 probe requests and every renewal (no NAK from the pair, longest gap 1.0 s), stayed in hot-standby, Kea and named were not restarted, a new lease was seen on both nodes afterwards. The security audit listed 6 findings on the generated RPZ zones before the upgrade and none after; the findings on the other zones were unchanged.
- **Slow node** (2 vCPU capped at 0.3 core): the security category disabled and enabled again with its 18 MB list on disk. Before the fix 1 change out of 3 was rolled back on "rndc status timed out"; with it 4 out of 4 applied (13 to 17 s), and at 0.15 core 2 out of 2 (30 and 34 s).
- **Network cuts in the browser**: the web port of a node refused connections for 8 s while a screen opened. An open session showed the error with **Retry** at 4 s and recovered by itself at 9 s; a screen whose code was not loaded yet reloaded and showed at 13 s.
- **Mixed pair amd64 and arm64 (emulated)**, Kea 2.6.3 from Debian in HA: 0.3.3 to 0.3.4 with `scripts/deploy-prod.sh`, arm64 standby first. Hot-standby kept, Kea not restarted, zones served, sign-in working.
- **Docker**: node and all-in-one images (ISC Kea 3.2.1 and Debian's Kea 2.6.3) built from the release tree; compose and all-in-one smoke tests pass, including a DHCP subnet plan written back by Kea under Docker's default AppArmor profile.
- Unit, frontend, end-to-end and daemon suites (BIND and Kea running for real, also in a container under Docker's default AppArmor profile).

## Upgrade
- **From 0.3.3**: `scripts/deploy-prod.sh --tag v0.3.4 <standby> <primary>` (or a downloaded release directory in place of `--tag`, docs/user/upgrade.md), or **Administration > Updates**.
- **Docker**: `ghcr.io/neyslim/pelagos:0.3.4` and `ghcr.io/neyslim/pelagos-allinone:0.3.4` (amd64 and arm64). These are the first images since 0.3.2: they also carry the fixes of 0.3.3 (DHCP changes under Docker's AppArmor profile).
- **RHEL 9 and 10**: the el9 and el10 rpms are attached to the release.
- This release is **not signed**: the Updates screen shows it as *Not signed* and asks for the extra confirmation. The sums of `SHA256SUMS` are checked in every case.
